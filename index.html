MAHAMA HIGHLIGHTS DIGITAL TAX REFORMS AS GRA OFFICERS MEET PRESIDENT

Accra, Ghana — Published in Politics & Economy

President John Dramani Mahama has reiterated his government's commitment to modernising Ghana's tax administration and improving the ease with which taxpayers interact with the Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA).

The President made the remarks during an engagement with officials of the Ghana Revenue Authority in Accra, where he highlighted the importance of digitalisation in making tax-related services more efficient and accessible to the public.

The engagement comes against the background of Ghana's ongoing efforts to expand digital tax services. The GRA has introduced electronic Tax Clearance Certificates, allowing taxpayers to apply for and receive their certificates through the Taxpayers' Portal. According to the Authority, the system is intended to reduce delays, lower compliance costs and help prevent fraudulent tax clearance certificates.

President Mahama, in the scenario, urged GRA officials to continue placing taxpayers at the centre of the country's revenue administration system. He said efficient tax collection would remain important to government's efforts to mobilise domestic revenue and finance national development.

Following the President's address, he proceeded to interact with a number of GRA officials who had gathered at the event. He exchanged brief handshakes with several members of staff as he moved through the group.

Among the officers was Mr. Emmanuel Wagka Brook, a Tax Clearance Officer with the Ghana Revenue Authority. Mr. Emmanuel Wagka Brook was one of many officers who had the opportunity to meet the President and exchange a handshake with him.

Although the interaction between President Mahama and Mr. Emmanuel Wagka Brook lasted only a few moments, it became a memorable experience for the officer. The handshake took place as part of the President's wider interaction with GRA personnel and was not a separate meeting or special appointment with Mr. Emmanuel Wagka Brook.

The engagement also highlighted the important role played by tax clearance officers and other GRA personnel in Ghana's revenue administration. Tax Clearance Certificates are used for several important transactions, including government tenders, certain importation activities and other official processes.

The government is expected to continue pursuing measures aimed at improving tax compliance, expanding digital services and making Ghana's tax system more efficient.
